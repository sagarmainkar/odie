### chart
For: A trend, a comparison, or parts of a whole.
Use when: line: trends. bar: periods or items. pie, donut: parts of a whole (budget split, portfolio mix). stacked: a total and its parts over time. hbar: rankings, long names. area: growth that builds up (a SIP). combo: two related measures (revenue and margin %).
Fields: kind: line | bar | pie | donut | stacked | hbar | area | combo. title. x: labels. series: [{name, data: numbers (null for a gap)}], 1-3 series (stacked, area: up to 6; pie, donut: 1). combo: bars for the first series, lines for the rest; a line in another unit adds its own unit: "%". unit: "%", "₹", "₹Cr", "$M", "pts" or a short word. source: optional site name.
